## Why

P2.6: parents pay for their children and should manage them in the portal without the children needing their own e-mail address.

## What Changes

- The payer of a household (by the payer's e-mail address) sees and manages the household's memberships in the portal like their own: data changes, invoices, documents.
- Cards say "Du verwaltest diese Mitgliedschaft als zahlende Person des Haushalts."
- Members with their own e-mail address resign themselves; the payer can resign only for members without one (e.g. children). The club still confirms every resignation.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `member-portal`: household proxy.

## Impact

No migration.
