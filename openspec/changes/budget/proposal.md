## Why

P3.8: the general assembly approves a budget (Haushaltsplan) and the board reports against it. Clubs keep it in spreadsheets next to the books.

## What Changes

- Planned net amount per income and expense account and year; compared with the booked net amounts of that year (income: credit − debit; expense: debit − credit).
- Buchhaltung → Haushaltsplan: plan per account (editable), actual, variance (red when spending more or earning less than planned), a court-line bar for plan use, totals; "Ist Vorjahr als Plan übernehmen" fills the plan from last year's actuals for review.
